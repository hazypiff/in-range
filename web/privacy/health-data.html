In Range Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Washington My Health My Data Act · Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Contact: privacy@inrange.life

This is a separate document from our Privacy Policy, as Washington law requires.

Why this document exists

In Range is a social discovery service — for dating, friends, and shared interests — not a health app, and we do not intend to collect health information. However, Washington's My Health My Data Act defines "consumer health data" broadly: personal information that identifies, or could reasonably infer, a person's past, present, or future physical or mental health. Because In Range processes location and proximity data, the places you go (for example, a clinic) or the people you are near could, in principle, allow such an inference. To the extent any data we hold is considered consumer health data under the Act, this policy governs it. The Act can apply to data collected in Washington even from visitors.

Categories we may be deemed to collect

  • Location data — precise GPS for up to 24 hours; coarse venue cells if you enable background location.
  • Proximity/encounter records — which members' phones were near yours, when, at neighborhood granularity.

In each case only to the extent an inference about health could be drawn from them. We do not collect any other category of health data.

Sources

Directly from your device, only with the consents described in our Privacy Policy — each consent is separate, purpose-scoped, and nothing is pre-selected.

Purposes

Confirming real-world encounters and suggesting people you may want to meet. We do not use consumer health data for any other purpose, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling for advertising, or analytics.

Sharing

None. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with any third party without your separate, affirmative authorization given for that specific disclosure — which we do not currently request, because we make no such disclosures. Legal compulsion (a valid court order) is the only exception.

Your rights

  • Access: confirmation of whether we hold consumer health data about you, and access to it.
  • Withdrawal: withdraw consent for its collection — Settings → consent toggles in the app, or by email. Withdrawing consent stops future collection.
  • Deletion: delete in the app or by email to privacy@inrange.life — see the deletion page. Deleting your account erases the data within the windows in our Privacy Policy; location and sightings purge within 24 hours in any case.

If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision. If the appeal is denied, you may raise a complaint with the Washington Attorney General.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be notified in advance. Contact: privacy@inrange.life.
