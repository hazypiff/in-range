In Range Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · Contact: privacy@inrange.life

In Range introduces you only to people your phone was physically near — for dating, new friends, and shared interests. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. If you live in Washington State, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which is a separate document.

The short version.

Your precise location is deleted from our servers within 24 hours.

The Bluetooth codes your phone broadcasts rotate constantly and contain no name, face, or location.

An encounter is recorded only when both phones agree it happened.

We never sell your data and we show no advertising.

You can delete everything — account and data — from the app or by email, no app required.

What we collect and why

Account and profile

Email address (sign-in), and the profile you create: name, date of birth, gender, who you're interested in, bio, and interests. Gender and romantic preference are sensitive information; we use them only to suggest people you might want to meet.

Photos

Profile photos are required to be shown to others. Location metadata (EXIF/GPS) is stripped from every photo before it leaves your phone. Photos are checked against our safety rules.

Bluetooth proximity signals

Your phone broadcasts a rotating anonymous code and listens for other members' codes. Codes rotate roughly every 15 minutes and are meaningless to anyone else. Signal sightings (the code seen, signal strength, time) are uploaded to confirm encounters.

Precise location (GPS)

Used to confirm an encounter really happened and to improve distance accuracy. Deleted from our servers within 24 hours, enforced by automated purge.

Background location (optional — off unless you enable it)

Occasional location checks while the app is closed so your phone can wake to detect nearby members and recognize venues. Only a coarse area (about a 3-mile cell) is stored — never a continuous trail — and checks stop the moment you turn the beacon off.

WiFi and venue signals

WiFi network identifiers (BSSIDs) are cryptographically hashed on your device and never uploaded; only coarse venue hints leave the phone.

Encounters

When both phones agree you crossed paths, we record the encounter — the time and a neighborhood-level place, never coordinates.

Messages

Messages you send other members, kept until you delete your account; the text of your messages is redacted when you delete.

Push tokens

A device token so we can notify you about matches and encounters (Apple Push Notification service on iOS, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android).

Waitlist (this website)

If you join the waitlist at inrange.life, we store your email, when you joined, your position and referral code, and which page section you came from. We do not store your IP address (rate-limit counters keep only a self-pruning hash). The site stores your email and referral code in your own browser's local storage to recognize you on return; that never reaches us beyond your signup.

Safety reports

If you file an intimate-image report, we keep the report, your email (used only to handle the report), and resolution records as the law requires.

What we do NOT do

No advertising. No sale or rental of personal data. No data brokers. No advertising SDKs and no cross-app tracking. No analytics built on your location: consent is purpose-scoped — consent for proximity matching does not cover analytics or advertising, and we collect no such consent because we do no such processing.

How encounters are verified (and why that's a privacy feature)

An encounter exists only when both phones agree: the two devices exchange rotating codes and independently confirm proximity with reciprocal evidence, with server-side abuse detection for relays and forged sightings. Nobody can invent an encounter with you, and nobody's feed contains anyone they weren't actually near.

Retention

  • Precise GPS: 24 hours, automated purge
  • Proximity sightings: 24 hours, automated purge
  • Rotating codes/token history: valid ~15–20 minutes; server copies purged within 24 hours
  • Encounters (neighborhood only): until you delete your account
  • Profile, photos, messages, email, push tokens: until you delete your account
  • Deleted accounts: content erased immediately; the account record is kept up to 30 days as protection against mistaken or malicious deletion, then permanently purged
  • Safety/legal records (e.g., intimate-image reports): as long as the law requires
  • Anonymous, de-identified statistics: indefinitely, never linked back to you

Who processes your data

Supabase (database, authentication, photo storage, server functions), Cloudflare (website hosting), Apple (app distribution, Push Notification service), and Google (Play distribution, Firebase Cloud Messaging). We do not otherwise share personal data except when the law requires it (for example, reports to NCMEC or law enforcement).

Your rights and choices

  • Access and export: download your data in the app (Settings → Download my data).
  • Correction: edit your profile in the app.
  • Consent withdrawal: every consent is a separate toggle in Settings — withdrawal is as easy as granting, applies immediately, and stopping Bluetooth proximity consent stops the beacon on your device, not just on our servers. We honor revocations within 15 days at the latest (most are instant).
  • Deletion: in the app (Settings → Delete my account), or by email to privacy@inrange.life from your account address — no app required. See what is erased and what is kept.
  • Pause instead: pausing hides you from new encounters and is reversible.
  • Waitlist only: email privacy@inrange.life and we'll remove you from the list.

Depending on your state, you may have additional statutory rights, including the right to appeal if we decline a request; we honor those and will tell you how to appeal in any denial.

Children

In Range is strictly 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we terminate accounts discovered to belong to minors.

Security

Rotating ephemeral identifiers, on-device hashing of WiFi identifiers, EXIF stripping, encrypted transport, row-level access controls on every table, and device-integrity protections (rolling out).

Changes and contact

We'll post changes here and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. Contact: privacy@inrange.life.
